# Shrine Process — Coordinator Handover

**The one rule: the process must not be missed on a single day.**
If you're ever unsure, the right move is whatever gets a named, contacted person at the shrines tomorrow.

One Brahmachari covers all 11 shrines for 3 days, then hands the kit to the next person. Four slots — 12 days — is one **batch**. The Shrine app holds the roster; you do the chasing.

---

## Your whole job is two repeating tasks

**Every 12 days, on the first day of a batch**
Tap that batch's header → copy **Email IDs** and **Heads-up message** → send to the four people in the *next* batch, CC the other coordinators. Type the subject yourself and put the dates in it: `Shrine Process Reminder — 12–23 Sep`.

**Every 3 days, on the day before a slot starts**
1. Open that person's card → copy **Day before reminder** → send on their channel.
2. Mark the **lamp** on the person whose slot just ended.
3. Look at the next batch — anyone without a thumbs-up gets chased today.

Put both in your task app. Nothing in the app will remind you.

---

## The three taps on a person's card

| Tap | Means |
|---|---|
| 👍 | They replied and confirmed they're available |
| 🔔 | They confirmed they have the kit |
| 🪔 | They completed the process — this is the only tap that updates their record |

Tap again to undo. Ticks are your notes; they notify nobody.

---

## Colours on the Schedule tab

Dark "TBD" = nobody assigned · ember = assigned, unconfirmed · brighter = confirmed · amber = has the kit · green = done · **red = the dates passed and nothing was marked.** Red means find out what happened today.

---

## Chasing, by the calendar

Counting back from the day a batch starts:

- **6 days before** — everyone should have a thumbs-up. Copy **Availability follow-up** and their contact detail, send it.
- **3 days before** — still silent? Phone them. Ask the other coordinators to try.
- **1 day before** — still silent? Replace them with a backup. Don't wait and hope.

Replacing clears all three ticks. The new person has confirmed nothing — chase from scratch.

---

## Empty slots

An empty slot inside the next 90 days outranks every reminder on your list.

Go to **List → Next up** (available people, longest since they last did it, at the top), tap the button on their row to copy their contact, tap **Copy scheduling link**, send it. They pick their own dates.

If you assign someone directly instead, **tell them the same minute**. Assigning notifies nobody, and an assigned person who doesn't know is worse than an empty slot — it looks filled.

If someone can't do this at all, mark them **Not available** in the List. Their future slots empty to TBD. Fill them that day.

---

## What the app won't do

It never sends anything — every message is copied and sent by you. It won't warn you about an empty slot or a silent person. The old Google Sheet is no longer live. And the lamp tick only means someone *said* they did it, so keep an eye on the shrines.

---

## Before you take over, get these

App link and passcode · the email thread and CC list · **where the kit is right now** · the backup pool names · today's list of TBD slots inside 90 days · who to escalate to if someone is unreachable.
