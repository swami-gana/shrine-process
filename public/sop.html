Shrine Process — Coordinator SOP

How to run the shrine process using the Shrine app · written 11 September 2026

The one rule: the shrine process must not be missed on a single day. Everything below exists only to serve that. If you are ever unsure what to do, the correct action is the one that gets a named, contactable person standing at the shrines tomorrow.

SECTION 1What you are taking over

Eleven shrines. One Brahmachari performs the process across all eleven for three days, then hands a physical kit to the next person. Four consecutive slots — twelve days — are treated as one batch, because that is the unit you send email about.

Your job is three things, in order of importance:

  1. Every slot has a name in it. An empty slot is a missed day waiting to happen.
  2. That person knows and has confirmed. People have been scheduled without their knowledge in the past, did not turn up, and complained. Do not schedule anyone silently.
  3. The kit gets handed over. Each person collects it from the person before and passes it to the person after.

SECTION 2The app, in one screen

Three tabs along the bottom. Swipe or tap between them.

  • List — every Brahmachari, their status, and their contact details.
  • Schedule — the roster. This is where you work.
  • Schedule Yourself — the booking view. This is the tab whose link you send out; it is also what Brahmacharis see.

On the Schedule tab:

  • 1 A tick on a batch header means all four people in that batch confirmed, collected the kit, and completed. The whole batch dims.
  • 2 A dimmed green slot is done and closed. Nothing to do.
  • 3 The orange outline marks the slot running today.
  • 4 Below it, the next batch — this is where all of your work is.
  • 5 Three taps per person, left to right: thumbs-up (they confirmed they are available), bell (they confirmed they collected the kit), lamp (they completed the process). Tap to mark, tap again to undo.

SECTION 3Your two recurring duties

Put both of these in Google Tasks. They are the whole job.

Every 12 days — on the first day of each batch

Send the heads-up email for the next batch. Section 4.

Every 3 days — on the day before each slot starts

Three things: send that person the day-before reminder, mark the outgoing person's lamp, and check whether anyone in the next batch still has no thumbs-up. Section 5.

Written against a batch that begins on day D:

WhenAction
D minus 12Heads-up email to all four, stakeholders on CC
D minus 6All four should have a thumbs-up. Call anyone who does not.
D minus 3Still unconfirmed? Raise it. Ask others to help reach them.
D minus 1Day-before reminder to the first person. If still unconfirmed, put a backup in.
D plus 2, 5, 8Reminder to the next person; mark the previous one's lamp
D plus 11Mark the fourth person's lamp. Batch closes.

SECTION 4Sending the heads-up email

One email to all four people in a batch, twelve days ahead. Tap the batch header to open its actions.

  1. Tap the batch header. Two rows appear.
  2. 1 Tap Email IDs. All four addresses are now on your clipboard. Paste into the To field in Gmail.
  3. 2 Tap Heads-up message. Paste into the body. It arrives already formatted, with the batch's four slots plus the slot before and after, so the first person knows whom to collect the kit from and the last knows whom to hand it to.
  4. Type the subject yourself: Shrine Process Reminder — 12–23 Sep. Always put the dates in. Four people on a thread with a generic subject will reply to the wrong batch.
  5. CC the other coordinators so everyone knows who is on duty.
  6. Send. Do not mark anything yet — the thumbs-up means they replied, not that you asked.

If a slot in that batch still reads TBD, the email will say TBD on that line. Fill the slot first (Section 6), then copy the message.

SECTION 5Chasing, reminding, and recording

Everything for one person lives behind their card. Tap the card to open it.

  • 1 Contact details — tap any row to copy that detail. Only the channels that person actually has are shown.
  • 2 Messages — tap to copy a message with their name, their dates, and the people before and after already filled in. Paste it into WhatsApp, SMS or email unchanged.
  • 3 Replace person — swaps in someone else. Section 7.

Chasing a confirmation

  1. Six days before the batch starts, look for any card without a thumbs-up.
  2. Open that card, copy Availability follow-up, copy their WhatsApp number or email, and send it.
  3. When they reply, tap the thumbs-up on their card.
  4. Three days before the batch, anyone still without a thumbs-up gets a phone call, not a message. If you cannot reach them, ask the other coordinators to try.
  5. One day before their slot, if they are still silent, replace them with a backup. Do not wait and hope.

The day before someone's slot

  1. Open their card, copy Day before reminder, send it on their channel.
  2. It tells them whom to collect the kit from and whom to hand it to. Both names are filled in automatically, including across a batch boundary.
  3. When they confirm they have the kit, tap the bell. The bell means the kit is in their hands — it is the single most useful thing on this screen, because a person with the kit will do the process.

When their three days are over

Tap the lamp on their card. This is the only tap that changes anything beyond the schedule: it records the date, and that person's entry in the List tab updates to "Last done N days ago", which is what drives who gets asked next. If you forget, the slot turns red. You can still tap the lamp later and it will go green.

SECTION 6Filling an empty slot

Two ways. Prefer the first — people who choose their own dates turn up.

Way one: ask people to book themselves

  1. Go to the List tab and tap 2 Next up. This shows only people who are available, ordered by who has gone longest without doing it. The top of that list is whom to ask.
  2. 3 Each row shows their status underneath their name.
  3. 4 Tap the button on the right to copy their best contact detail. The badge tells you which channel it is, so you know where to paste it — WA for WhatsApp, SMS for phone.
  4. 1 Tap Copy scheduling link at the top and send them the link. They pick their own slot and you get an email when they do.

Way two: assign someone directly

  1. On the Schedule tab, tap the TBD card.
  2. Tap Select person, search their name, confirm.
  3. Now tell them. Assigning does not notify anyone. Copy their contact detail and their availability follow-up and send it the same minute you assign them. An assigned person who does not know is worse than an empty slot, because it looks filled.

Keep three months filled. If there is an empty slot inside the next 90 days, that is the most urgent thing on your plate, ahead of any reminder.

SECTION 7Replacing someone, and marking people unavailable

  1. To replace: open the person's card on the Schedule tab, tap Replace person, pick the new name, confirm.
  2. Replacing clears all three marks. The new person has confirmed nothing. Start the chase again from scratch — copy the availability follow-up and send it immediately.
  3. If someone tells you they can no longer do this at all — travelling, or not physically able — open their row in the List tab and tap Mark not available. Every future slot they hold empties to TBD, and the app tells you how many. Fill those slots the same day.
  4. Marking them available again restores their previous status, but does not put them back into the slots that were emptied.

Emergencies

There is a small backup pool — people held off the regular roster for exactly this. Find them with the Backup filter on the List tab, call the one who has gone longest without doing it, and use Replace person once they agree.

SECTION 8The Schedule Yourself tab

This is the view Brahmacharis get. Check it after you send the link out.

  • 1 An outlined card is open for booking.
  • 2 A plain card is taken, with the name of whoever holds it.

Scroll it occasionally to see how far ahead the roster is actually filled. You get an email each time someone books, but the tab is the truth.

SECTION 9What the app does not do

  • It never sends a message. Every message is copied by you and sent by you. Nothing goes out because you tapped something.
  • Marking a tick tells nobody. The ticks are your record, not a notification.
  • It does not watch the schedule for you. Nothing will alert you to an empty slot or an unconfirmed person. That is what the two recurring tasks in Section 3 are for.
  • The Google Sheet is no longer live. It was read once at setup. Keep it as an archive if you like, but the app is the record now.
  • Verify at the shrines. The lamp tick means someone told you they did it. Keep an eye on the shrines themselves.

SECTION 10If something looks wrong

As of 11 September 2026 the app is still being fixed. Two things you may hit:

  • A bar at the top saying a change was not saved. Your change is still on screen and still queued. Tap Retry when you have signal. If it persists, take a screenshot and send it on.
  • Ticks appearing or disappearing on their own. This is a known bug under repair. Until it is confirmed fixed, reload the app and re-check the batch you are working on before you trust what you see, and keep your own note of what you have sent.

Anything else — a name that will not save, a slot that will not fill, a message that copies blank — screenshot it and pass it back rather than working around it.

SECTION 11Handover checklist

  1. App link, and the coordinator passcode if one is set.
  2. The two recurring tasks from Section 3, in your own task app.
  3. Access to the email thread and the CC list of other coordinators.
  4. Where the physical kit currently is, and who holds it.
  5. Names of the backup pool.
  6. Which slots inside the next 90 days are still TBD, today.
  7. Who to escalate to if a person cannot be reached at all.
